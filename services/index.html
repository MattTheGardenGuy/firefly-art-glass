Classes & Services

Three glass crafts, private parties, team events, and open studio time — all taught by Debbie. No experience needed.

Every class is hands-on from the moment you sit down. Debbie guides your project — offers ideas, shows you techniques, keeps you from common mistakes — but the work is yours. You leave with something you actually made.

Glass Art Classes

All levels welcome. Drop-ins available. Call ahead for custom projects.

🔥
Most Popular
Fused Glass

Layer pieces of colored glass and fire them in the kiln. Make plates, bowls, ornaments, suncatchers, and art panels. Debbie's specialty — the class most people book twice.

🪟
Traditional Craft
Stained Glass

Learn to cut and solder glass into panels and suncatchers. A meditative skill with beautiful results. Debbie teaches the full process from cutting to soldering.

🪄
No Cutting Required
Mosaic Glass

Arrange glass pieces on mirrors, frames, and objects. Endless color combinations. A great entry point if you want to work with glass without cutting.

📖
Walk-In Welcome
Open Studio

No appointment needed during open hours. Pick a project from the available options and create at your own pace. Perfect for a solo afternoon or a spontaneous outing.

For Sale In-Studio
Handcrafted Gifts

Glass bowls, ornaments, panels, and sculptures made in-studio. Nothing mass-produced. Every piece is made by hand — by Debbie or by a student.
