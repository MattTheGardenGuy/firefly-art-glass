What We Create

Three ways to work with glass

Our instructor Debbie guides you through your project. You leave with something you made. No experience necessary.

🔥
Most Popular
Fused Glass

Layer pieces of colored glass and fire them in the kiln. Make plates, bowls, ornaments, and art panels. The most popular class — Debbie's specialty.

No experience needed
🪟
Traditional Craft
Stained Glass

Cut and solder glass into panels and suncatchers. A meditative, timeless skill.

🪄
Colorful & Tactile
Mosaic Glass

Arrange glass on mirrors, frames, and objects. Endless color combinations, no cutting required.

🛍️
Take Home
Handcrafted Gifts

Bowls, ornaments, sculptures made in-studio. Nothing mass-produced.

📖
Drop-In
Open Studio

No appointment needed. Show up, pick a project, create. Perfect for solo time or bringing a friend.
