Meet the Studio

Firefly Art Glass is Debbie's studio — and she's the reason people keep coming back. Patient, creative, and genuinely excited about glass.

Debbie — Instructor & Owner

Firefly Art Glass is a working studio in downtown Elkhorn, WI. Debbie opened it because she wanted a space where people could slow down, use their hands, and create something they're proud of.

She teaches every class herself — fused glass, stained glass, and mosaic. No pre-made kits, no shortcuts. You actually learn something while you're here, and you leave with a finished piece.

The studio draws everyone: first-timers who've never touched glass, retirees looking for a new hobby, bachelorette parties, corporate groups, couples on date nights, and families looking for something different to do together.

Debbie's approach: she guides your project without taking it over. It's your work, your vision — she just keeps you from cutting yourself.

Three Crafts, One Instructor

🔥
Fused Glass

Debbie's specialty. Plates, bowls, ornaments, art panels fired in the kiln. The most popular class.

🪟
Stained Glass

Cut and solder panels and suncatchers. A meditative, timeless skill taught hands-on.

🪄
Mosaic Glass

Arrange glass on mirrors, frames, and objects. No cutting required — all creativity, no stress.
